4. Special-category and biometric data: the optional "be the protagonist" feature

We are planning an optional, future face-personalization feature that lets you appear as the protagonist of a story. Facial geometry derived from an image you submit is biometric data and is a special category under GDPR Article 9, and may be a biometric identifier under US laws such as the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act (BIPA) and comparable state statutes.

Explicit, separate opt-in consent

This feature is off by default. We will only process facial/biometric data after you give explicit, separate, written opt-in consent presented apart from this policy and from general terms. You can decline and still use the rest of the service. You may withdraw consent at any time.

Strict purpose limitation

Facial data is used solely to generate your personalized frames for stories you choose. It is never used for identification, advertising, training of general-purpose models, sale, or any unrelated purpose.

5. Automated adaptation and profiling

Axessplayer adapts and re-cuts the story for each viewer. In line with the EU AI Act and GDPR transparency expectations, we disclose:

  • Adaptation happens. The cut, pacing, branch, or ending you see may differ from another viewer's, based on an inspectable, named-feature model (we use interpretable named features rather than opaque signals).
  • There is a control group. Some sessions are assigned to a control condition so we can measure the effect of adaptation. Your control-vs-treatment assignment and decision_id are recorded.
  • Your rights. This adaptation does not produce legal or similarly significant effects on you. Even so, you may ask for information about the logic involved, request a non-personalized experience, object to profiling based on consent, and withdraw consent for personalization at any time. Where you withdraw, we serve a default, non-adapted cut.
