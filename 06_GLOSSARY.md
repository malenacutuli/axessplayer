# Glossary

- Adaptive cinema: one production that re-cuts itself per viewer in real time.
- Series: the overarching IP, made of beats.
- Beat: an atomic narrative unit. The spine is the ordered sequence of beats.
- Beat variant: a playable version of a beat along axes: language, accessibility, intensity, POV, tier.
- Beat edge: a possible transition from one beat to another, with conditions.
- Branch point: a beat with more than one possible successor variant.
- Emotional graph / viewer_state: the per-viewer preference model that drives decisions.
- Decision: the choice of which variant to serve next. Made by the decision service, sub-50ms.
- Director's cut: the default fixed path. Served to opt-out users, on errors, and to the control group.
- Control / holdout: viewers who always get the director's cut, for measuring adaptive lift.
- Cold open: the interactive first scene that calibrates the viewer vector.
- Tier A / B / C: filmed (A), AI from consented likeness (B), pure AI for localization and connective beats (C).
- Dynamic product placement (DPP): filling a tagged in-scene slot with the right brand per cohort at serve time.
- Seamless branching runtime: the client and manifest tech that switches variants with no visible seam or buffer.
- Walking skeleton: the first thin end-to-end path that proves the system works.
- Coin / credit: the virtual currency. Earned, bought, or granted by subscription. Spent on unlocks and premium variants.
- Provenance / C2PA: cryptographic content credentials proving how a video was made.
