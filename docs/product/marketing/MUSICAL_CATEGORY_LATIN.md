# The Musical category: vertical musical microdrama for Latin Pop and musicians

**A new content category and an artist partnership playbook. June 2026. No em dashes, no emojis.**

Context: Second Rodeo and Holywater announced Playback, the first musical produced for vertical video, a songwriter
whose secret AI double becomes a global pop sensation, premiering on My Drama. The format is validated. The plot is,
almost word for word, the consent and provenance problem Axessplayer is built to solve. This is our opening.

## 1. The category in one line
Short, vertical, serialized musical drama built around original songs, where the music is the emotional engine and
the artist is the star, with the artist's likeness and voice consented, signed, and owned. Hook in the first three
seconds, a song per episode, a cliffhanger at the end, made for the phone.

## 2. Why Latin Pop and musicians, and why now
- Latin music is among the most streamed and most mobile native music on earth: reggaeton, regional Mexican, Latin
  pop, and the crossover wave drive global charts and short video culture.
- Artists already live on vertical video and already release serialized, fan driven content. A musical microdrama is
  the natural next format, not a stretch.
- The fan relationship is intense and monetizable: streams, merch, tickets, brand deals, fan communities. A musical
  series is a new top of funnel for all of them.
- The format is proven (Playback) and there is no consent first, accessible, artist owned home for it yet. That gap
  is ours.

## 3. The Axessplayer difference for an artist (the pitch)
Playback's plot is the fear: an AI version of you takes over your voice and identity. Axessplayer is the answer.
- Consent and provenance: the artist's likeness and voice are used only under a signed consent ledger entry, every

- One upload, every market: the artist records once and reaches every language and every screen.

## 4. How a musical series is built (format spec)
- Length: episodes of about 60 to 180 seconds, 8 to 24 episodes per season, daily or twice weekly drops.
- Music: one original or catalog song anchors each episode; the song and the story beat are written together so the
  hook lands musically and narratively at once.
- Star modes: the artist can appear as themselves (filmed or generation assisted), as a consented digital double for
  scale, or as the voice behind an animated lead. All three are consent gated and signed.
- Accessibility tracks: emotive captions, a sign language performance track, and localized audio, on by default.
- Provenance: every asset signed and labeled by the time it ships.

## 5. Monetization for the artist
- Consumer: coins, episode unlocks, subscriptions, rewarded ads on the series.
- Catalog lift: the series is a discovery engine that pushes fans to stream the songs on the DSPs, where the artist
  already earns.
- Brand rail: a brand lives in scene in the artist's world, consented and brand safe, a new sponsorship line that is
  not a banner.
- Merch and tickets: the series is the storefront and the tour trailer.
- Splits: 70 percent to the artist and rights holders on the audited ledger, with the math shown.

## 7. Original series concepts (IP clean, artist agnostic, ready to pitch)
These are original premises an artist can anchor. No existing show or real person is referenced in the fiction.
1. The Double. A rising artist consents to an AI version of themselves to keep up with demand, then has to prove to
   the world, and themselves, which voice is real. The consent positive answer to Playback, with the artist as hero.
2. Regional Heart. A regional Mexican family band fights to keep their sound as fame and a label try to change it.
   A saga of loyalty, corridos, and reinvention.
3. Perreo Royale. Two reggaeton rivals from the same barrio are forced into one track that becomes the song of the
   summer, and one of them is hiding who really wrote it.
4. The Feature. An unknown singer ghostwrites a megastar's biggest hit and has ten episodes to claim it before the
   awards show.
5. Encadenados. A telenovela style musical of a love triangle told entirely through duets, each episode a new song.
6. La Academia Vertical. A talent show thriller where the votes are rigged by an algorithm, and the contestants
   discover the data is the real villain. A wink at the platform's own values.

## 8. Target artist segments for outreach (illustrative, not commitments)
Approach by segment, not by chasing one name. Example archetypes and the angle that lands:
- Global reggaeton or Latin pop superstar: the consent and ownership story plus a new format to own before rivals.
- Regional Mexican breakout: a saga format that fits the genre's storytelling, plus global reach via localization.
- Crossover Latin pop artist: accessibility and 40 language reach to grow beyond the core market.
- Emerging viral artist: a low cost, generation assisted series to turn a viral moment into owned IP and a fanbase.
- Legacy artist seeking a new audience: a digital double, consented, to reach a mobile generation without touring.
Named targets are a business development decision; the proposal template below customizes to any of them. Do not
imply any artist has agreed until they have signed.
