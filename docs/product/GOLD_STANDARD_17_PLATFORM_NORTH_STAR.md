# Gold standard 17: platform North Star (canonical)

**The definitive platform definition for the build team. June 2026. No em dashes.**

The full prose version is the Definitive Platform Blueprint (Cannes deliverable). This is the canonical, agent
readable summary. It supersedes nothing in CORRECTIONS; it sets direction and adds the reading surface.

## The thesis
Axessplayer is the adaptation layer of entertainment: one story a person can READ, then WATCH in a version made for
them, with a brand that belongs in the world, all of it accessible, consented, and provable. Built in June 2026 as a
last mover: frontier video models, long context LLMs, mature C2PA, and the EU AI Act all arrived at once, so we build
the version the market requires next without the incumbents' legacy.

## Three surfaces, one spine
- READ: serialized fiction (Hello Sunshine curation x BookTok discovery x Inkitt data). Cheap to produce, fast to
  test. Where IP is born and demand is measured.
- WATCH: the adaptive vertical player. Same story re cut per viewer by language, pace, POV, intensity, accessibility,
  and in scene brand.
- CREATE: write or upload, test demand, adapt winners to adaptive video; accessibility, localization, consent,
  provenance automatic; keep 70 percent; own the IP.
- SPINE (the moat, shared by all three): variant substrate (text + video), decision and outcome engine
  (propensity + control + reward), accessibility on by default, consent/provenance/AI labeling, the brand rail.

## The flywheel (the most important loop in the company)
Read (cheap text demand test, owned IP) -> Adapt (graduate only proven stories to expensive adaptive video) ->
Measure (per viewer outcome memory) -> Monetize (coins/subs/ads + consented brand rail + enterprise) -> Reinvest
(all data feeds the next story, cut, and placement). Holywater proved the core: 30 percent of My Drama hits are
adapted from My Passion e books. We make the loop explicit, own every step, and add per viewer measurement and
consent, which they lack.

## Architecture rule
One substrate for text and video. The reading content model (works, chapters, installments) is a sibling of the
video beat and variant model, so a story graduates text to video inside one data structure. The existing engagement
and outcome pipeline is pointed at reading behavior to become the demand sensor. Reuse the consent, provenance,
accessibility, economy, and brand services unchanged. NO new heavy infrastructure ahead of real scale (no Kafka,
Neo4j, ClickHouse). Coherence is the small team's advantage over accreted incumbents.

## The moat (five, on one stack; no competitor holds more than two)
Accessibility (first class product + unique signal, 13T audience) | Trust (consent, provenance, EU sovereignty) |
Adaptation + outcome memory (per viewer matched record a fixed cut rival cannot generate) | The brand rail
(consented, in scene, measurable, generation native) | The reading funnel (owned IP origination + demand sensor).

## Competitive position (June 2026)
Holywater: ecosystem leader (~150M rev, FOX, read to video funnel); we hold accessibility, consent, EU, per viewer.
ReelShort/DramaBox: scale leaders in licensed drama; we hold owned IP, adaptation, accessibility, brand rail.
IDILIO + Shorta: two funded LatAm content plays (Shorta has elite founders, 6M seed); do NOT fight Spanish micro
drama head on, win the layer above. Wattpad/Inkitt: mature reading + publishing pipeline; we add adaptation to
accessible per viewer video and accessible first reading (Radish shut Dec 2025, talent and readers in motion).
Fable/Showrunner: AI TV sandbox; we add consent, labeling, reliability, a real product.

## Sequencing (ambition with discipline)
Build the reading platform NOW as a real product, but scoped as the demand sensor and IP origination engine, not a
sprawling Wattpad clone, so it feeds and de risks the video engine instead of splitting focus. Order: (1) keystone
data work (prompt 27) + reading MVP demand sensor; (2) Gate A lift number on a graduated story + first brand pilots
+ founding creators; (3) brand marketplace, full studio, editorial/archive adaptation, accessible reading at scale;
(4) scale, enterprise licensing, EU compliance as product, full read to watch flywheel. The binding constraint is
audience and the first proof of lift, not more surfaces. Reading is permitted now only because, scoped right, it
accelerates that proof.

## Honest caveats
Lift is unmeasured until the first live cohort (the reading funnel de risks content choice, not lift). Two products
is real risk, justified only as the demand sensor with the keystone kept first. The field is crowded and funded; the
answer is the layer above, not a content war.
